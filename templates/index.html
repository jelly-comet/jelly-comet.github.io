    About JellyComet

    JellyComet makes small, free audio plugins for Linux. Each does one job with one unusual control: a jellyfish pad for vocal pitch correction, an orbiting puck for loop effects, and white keys that play any scale or chord. All are CLAP plugins for x86-64 Linux, MIT-licensed, each with a manual.
