OrbitLoopBtw

One loop played four ways at once, half speed, reversed, stuttered and braked like a tape, and one puck that blends them.

For fills, breakdowns and endings that move with the song.

JellyPitchBtw

Pitch correction for one voice, steered by a jellyfish.

One puck sets how fast a note is pulled in and how much of the singing round it is left alone, and a display shows the voice as sung and as corrected.

EasyModeBtw

Play any scale on the white keys, as chords, strums or arpeggios.

A MIDI effect for Linux: the white keys play the notes of the scale you pick with its root on middle C, and each key can play a chord of the scale instead, strummed like a guitar or broken into an arpeggio on the beat.

Questions

Each plugin's manual has every control in full.

Read the OrbitLoopBtw manual

How do I install a plugin?

Unpack the archive, copy the .clap file into ~/.clap and rescan plugins in your DAW.

Which hosts load them?

Any CLAP host on Linux, such as Bitwig Studio and REAPER.

Is there a VST3 or a Windows build?

No. Releases are the Linux CLAP only, for x86_64.

Do the plugins use the network?

No. None of them goes online or sends telemetry.

How do I check a download?

Every release has a .sha256 file beside the archive. Run sha256sum -c on it.
