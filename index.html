JellyComet

Hand-made by tentacles, free for humans.

Glowing little audio tools, built to nudge you somewhere you didn't plan to go.

  • JellyPitchBtw

    v1.3.0 · 14 Nov 2026

    Pitch correction for one voice, steered by a jellyfish. One puck sets how fast a note is pulled in and how far off its note a voice may be and be left alone, and a display shows the voice as sung and as corrected.

    Download 5.3 MB Manual PDF
    Everything about JellyPitchBtw

  • OrbitLoopBtw

    v1.0.1 · 14 Nov 2026

    One loop played four ways at once, half speed, reversed, stuttered and braked like a tape, and one puck that blends them. For fills, breakdowns and endings that move with the song. CLAP for Linux.

    Download 5.4 MB Manual PDF
    Everything about OrbitLoopBtw

  • EasyModeBtw

    v1.2.6 · 14 Nov 2026

    Play any scale on the white keys, as chords, strums or arpeggios. A MIDI effect for Linux: the white keys play the notes of the scale you pick with its root on middle C, and each key can play a chord of the scale instead, strummed like a guitar or broken into an arpeggio on the beat.
